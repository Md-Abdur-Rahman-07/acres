Studio Q&A

Inside the studio

Can I ask about a custom piece?

Yes. Tell us about the shape, colors, and occasion you have in mind through our studio contact form.

How does the shopping bag work?

Choose a color and add a piece to your bag. Your selection stays in this browser tab for the session. You can send the studio an inquiry about the pieces you’ve chosen.

How should I care for my piece?

Handle glazed surfaces gently and ask the studio about food use, washing, and heat exposure for your particular piece.
